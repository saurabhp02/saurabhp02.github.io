A little about me

I am an Electronics and Communication Engineering student at NIT Nagaland, interested in embedded systems, robotics, IoT and intelligent hardware solutions.

I enjoy developing prototypes by combining microcontrollers, sensors, wireless communication and machine learning. With industrial internship exposure in electronics manufacturing, assembly procedures and testing workflows at Elexoplus, my interests include drone systems, safety-focused electronics and connected production-ready devices.

For me, the most valuable part of a project is the process: understanding the problem, building a solution, testing it in practice, debugging failures and improving reliability.
