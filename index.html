Robotics · Machine learning

Aerial Fire Detection & Suppression Drone

An RC drone prototype that detects fire with a machine-learning model and carries a fire-suppression payload concept.

Problem
Fires are found late and are dangerous to approach on foot.
Solution
ML-based fire detection with GPS and IMU sensing on an RC drone, plus an immediate-suppression payload concept.

IoT · Vehicle safety

MineSafe-X

A cooperative safety system that helps mine vehicles avoid collisions in fog and low visibility.

Problem
Drivers cannot see other vehicles in fog and low-visibility conditions.
Solution
Vehicle-to-vehicle communication over ESP-NOW, combined with GPS, radar and proximity sensing, with a live command-tower dashboard.
ESP32 ESP-NOW GPS mmWave IoT
Electronics · Safety

AC Line Fault Detection Device

A prototype that detects abnormal conditions in AC electrical lines and gives a simple indication of a possible fault.

Problem
Faults in AC lines are hard to notice and can lead to equipment damage or safety risks.
Solution
A sensing and indication circuit that flags abnormal line conditions.
