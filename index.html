About Me

Engineering with a hands-on approach.

My interests lie at the intersection of hardware, software and intelligent systems.

I am an engineering student at NIT Nagaland with a strong interest in electronics, embedded systems, robotics and emerging technologies.

My project work focuses on building practical prototypes using microcontrollers, sensors, communication systems, artificial intelligence and IoT technologies.
