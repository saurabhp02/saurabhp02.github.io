2nd Place · Wildcard Track

Ostereo-AI — Runner-Up, Code For Nagaland 2026

NEXUSCIPHERGUARD INDIA in collaboration with ICFAI University Nagaland

Secured second place in the Wildcard Track at Code For Nagaland 2026, a 26-hour hackathon featuring 113 students from eight colleges and universities, with a total prize pool of ₹1 lakh.

Our team developed an AI-Based Osteoarthritis Risk Marker Screening System designed to support early risk screening, particularly in remote and resource-constrained healthcare settings. The proposed solution combines symptom assessment, walking-pattern analysis and AI-assisted medical image analysis to identify potential risk markers and support timely medical referral.

The challenge tested our technical knowledge, teamwork, rapid prototyping and problem-solving under tight deadlines. This achievement reflects our commitment to applying artificial intelligence to meaningful healthcare challenges.
