Healthcare AI · Multimodal screening

Osterio – AI-Based Osteoarthritis Risk Marker Screening System

An AI-assisted screening platform that combines patient symptoms, walking-video analysis, sensor data and knee X-ray analysis to identify potential osteoarthritis risk markers, with a focus on accessible screening in remote and low-resource healthcare settings.

Problem
People in remote communities may have limited access to specialist assessment and diagnostic facilities for early osteoarthritis screening.
Solution
Combines a symptom questionnaire, camera-based gait analysis, IMU and FSR sensor readings, and AI-assisted knee X-ray analysis to generate screening findings and a digital report for healthcare review.
Application
Designed to support preliminary screening and referral in clinics, rural healthcare centres and community health camps. It is not a replacement for clinical diagnosis.
